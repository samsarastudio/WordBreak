Separate connected tires

Fit each cylinder around the tire and rim, keeping the fender and suspension outside. Orange marks the selected wheel. Dimensions are metres.

Covers paint bleed on inward-facing tire surfaces. Outer rims and tread keep their textures.

Left and right are from the driver's seat. Front is positive Z. These are suggested regions; inspect all four before cutting.
